The Big Story · The List Is Out

ECONOMIC OUTCAST LANDS IN DUBAI'S BACKYARD: SEVEN UAE SHIPPING ENTITIES ON THE LIST

The OFAC list is readable and it runs through this city: seven UAE entities designated including Good Luck Shipping (Dubai), Ship Fuels and Trade DMCC, Shipoil FZCO, Target Horizon Shipping and Foscom FZE (Ajman), alongside Dubai based brokers "Captain Hamzah" and Ivan Obukhov, six tankers, the Wellbred trading network (Singapore, UAE, Switzerland) and new sectoral determinations for aviation, shipping, gold, technology and digital assets (OFAC ↗, gCaptain ↗). China was spared, which is why Brent sits calmly at $92.16, but Bessent promised "a major announcement of a financial institution being sanctioned by the end of this week" (Argus ↗). Iran's rial hit a record 2.02 million per dollar; Atlantic Council experts call the package "a warning shot" whose force depends on Beijing (AC ↗).

Why it matters: screen today's client, agent and bunkering lists against the seven named UAE entities and the tanker IMOs before close of business; any historical touchpoint needs documenting now. The aviation sectoral determination means Iran linked parts, leasing and handling chains are formally in scope. And the promised bank designation is this week's binary event: if it is Chinese, everything reprices.
