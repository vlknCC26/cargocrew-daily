The Big Story · 48 Hours Against The Hammer

IRAN PUTS A TWO DAY HORMUZ REOPENING PLAN ON THE TABLE AND OIL FALLS 2% THE SAME MORNING, WHILE TRUMP TALKS OF "THE HAMMER" AND AXIOS REPORTS NETANYAHU PRESSED HIM TO STRIKE THE MOMENT THE MIDTERMS CLOSE: BOTH ENDGAMES ARE NOW FULLY PRICED INTO THE NEXT 72 HOURS

The reply window produced a plan rather than a reply. Araqchi, after the Doha consultations, says Iran has compressed its seven day Hormuz reopening sequence to 48 hours: the US takes agreed measures on day one, the strait reopens and transit returns to its prior state on day two (Gulf News, updated 02:49 this morning). The attached demands are the known set: lift the recent sanctions, reinstate the June oil waivers, recognise the Iran and Oman designated routes, and end the conflict on all fronts. Against it stands the other track, sharpened overnight: Trump told an Iowa rally "at some point we're going to have to give them the hammer" and said remaining targets, Pickaxe Mountain named, could be hit "within minutes" (AFP via Arab News), while Axios reports via two sources that Netanyahu pressed Trump in a "tough" Saturday call to strike immediately after tomorrow's midterms, with Trump preferring to let the negotiations run (JPost carrying Axios adds, UNVERIFIED, 10+ US transport aircraft into Israel this week and interceptors moving to Israel and Arab allies). Why it matters: for the first time since the strait closed there is a dated, 48 hour mechanism for reopening it, and the oil market moved on it before breakfast. But the same weekend put the hardest strike pressure yet on the other side of the scale, with a named date (the day after tomorrow) and an ally pushing for it. Every quote CargoCrew writes today should assume the binary resolves this week: hold 24 hour validity, keep both scenarios priced, and treat any US response to the two day plan, or any strike, as the repricing event. The 2% oil drop is the preview of what a yes does; it is not yet a trend.
