The Big Story · The A340 Fleet Lives

STODDART CLOSES THE EUROPEAN CARGO RESCUE TWO DAYS EARLY: 16 A340s BACK IN PLAY

Paul Stoddart's European Aviation Group completed the acquisition of all European Cargo assets from the administrators on Tuesday, ahead of the expected Thursday announcement: 16 flight ready A340s plus Trent engine spares, with operations continuing from Bournemouth ("I feel totally confident that we can keep this excellent fleet of cargo aircraft flying", DV ↗). The carrier flew China to UK e-commerce before its June administration cut 174 of 219 staff (ACN ↗). A revived widebody operator now needs customers, routes and commercial partners, fast.

Why it matters: this is the rare radar lead that just converted from watch file to live target. Sixteen A340s of cheap widebody capacity re entering a market where MESA yields run +47% YoY: be the first Middle East commercial call the new owner takes. The introduction goes out today, not after they staff up.
