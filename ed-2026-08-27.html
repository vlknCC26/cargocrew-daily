The Big Story · The €3 Duty Redraws the Map

CHINA TO EUROPE FREIGHTER CAPACITY IS DOWN NEARLY 30%, AND IT IS NOT COMING BACK SOON

Rotate data shows China to Europe freighter capacity still 28% below June levels two months after the EU's €3 duty on low value parcels, with July e-commerce imports down 24% MoM. The gateway carnage is concentrated: Madrid down 78%, Ürümqi down 72%, Budapest down 58%, Liège down 35% (ACN ↗). The same policy shock shows up in the BAI print: out of China, rates firmed to Europe on thin capacity while softening to the US (ACN ↗).

Why it matters: structural capacity exit is what keeps rates up in a soft demand market, and displaced freighters have to fly somewhere: Gulf, sea air and secondary EU gateways are the beneficiaries. For CargoCrew this validates the DWC sea air pitch and makes displaced capacity operators (including the parked A340s and A321Fs on the radar) natural partners for redeployment.
