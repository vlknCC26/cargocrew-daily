The Big Story · Day Nineteen

THE FED DECIDES TONIGHT, INTO A JET FUEL MARKET THAT HAS DOUBLED IN A YEAR

Decision day. At ~18:00 UTC the FOMC delivers what markets price at ~85%: the first rate hike since 2023, 25 points off a 3.50 to 3.75% base, with the wildcard that Chair Warsh himself has floated holding on cooling core CPI. The backdrop hardened overnight: Brent settled $108.75, up 2.9%, dated Brent cleared $130 for the first time since April, Shanghai crude printed a record, and US diesel hit $6.27 a gallon. The IATA split confirms what every fuel clause is about to feel: global jet fuel $181.46/bbl, up 101.6% in a year, on a Brent basis of $113.66 that jumped 15.1% in a week. The one soft spot is the data: TAC's BAI edged just +0.2% WoW globally, and TAC itself notes the fuel surge is not yet reflected in rates. Meanwhile the ballistic pause reached day seven, though Iran put five missiles into Iraqi Kurdistan on the Mahsa Amini anniversary, and CENTCOM broke six days of silence to confirm destroying two IRGC boats that tried to seize a US naval drone (Kip ↗ · GS ↗ · ACW ↗).

Why it matters: the gap between a fuel index up 101.6% and a rate index up 20.2% is margin someone is about to lose, and TAC has just told the whole market the repricing is still ahead. Sell forward today at indexed rates before the surcharge wave breaks; hold every long dated commitment until tonight's statement and the December signal are in; the cost of money and the cost of fuel are rising together.
