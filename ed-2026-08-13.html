The Big Story · Hub Realignment

ISTANBUL DETHRONES FRANKFURT AS EUROPE'S BUSIEST CARGO AIRPORT

The war has redrawn Europe's cargo map: IST handled just over 1m tonnes in H1 (+10.8%) against Frankfurt's 980,731t (+1.3%), as volumes divert from Gulf hubs and Turkish Cargo rides the shift — Turkish volumes +11.3%, cargo revenue +58% to ~$1.3bn (ACI Europe data via ACN ↗). Frankfurt's drag: weak Eurozone, April's Lufthansa strike, and the still-grounded A321F fleet. Meanwhile Iran declared the strait "remains blocked" — transits are down ~90% at ~13/day (CNBC ↗) — so the diversion flows feeding IST aren't reversing soon.

Why it matters: transshipment share is migrating to Istanbul while the Gulf is constrained — a competitive risk to Dubai's hub position, but also a play: build IST routing options into CargoCrew's ME-workaround products, and treat Turkish-market coverage as a strategic gap worth closing.
