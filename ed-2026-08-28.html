The Big Story · The Cost, Counted

JEBEL ALI FALLS OUT OF THE WORLD'S TOP 30 BOX PORTS FOR THE FIRST TIME IN 20 YEARS

The Gulf crisis now has a headline number: Jebel Ali dropped from 10th to 32nd in the H1 port rankings, handling 3.14m TEU against 7.77m a year earlier, with Q2 down more than 90% to just 374,000 TEU on the Hormuz near shutdown; Abu Dhabi's Khalifa fell out of the top 50 entirely. The boxes went to Colombo (+11.9%) and Nhava Sheva (+13.6%) as carriers rerouted around the Gulf (Loadstar ↗).

Why it matters: this is the sea side of the story CargoCrew has been living since February, and it cuts both ways. The threat: Dubai's transshipment gravity is leaking to Colombo and Nhava Sheva, and some of it will not come back. The opportunity: every box that cannot sail is a candidate for air or sea air, and dnata's new command centre (below) plus Dubai Customs' fast track give the air side a service story the sea side cannot match right now. Put the Jebel Ali number in every pitch this month.
