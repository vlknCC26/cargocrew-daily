The Big Story · The Home Market Moves

FLYDUBAI GOES FREIGHTER, ON SOLITAIR'S METAL: THREE 737-800Fs FROM 1 OCTOBER

The biggest structural change in Dubai's cargo market this year landed on a Friday. flydubai launches dedicated freighter operations on 1 October with three 737-800Fs wet leased from SolitAir, based at DWC with 23 tonne payloads, selling main deck capacity and charters across a network of more than 125 destinations spanning Africa, Central Asia, the Caucasus, the GCC and South and Southeast Asia; CCO Hamad Obaidalla pitches guaranteed capacity, and 787 conversions are flagged from 2029. It comes as the macro backdrop hardened: August CPI printed +0.4% with core overshooting at +0.3%, sending Fed hike odds from about 70% to near 90% for the decision now confirmed for Wednesday 16 September (a calendar correction: the announcement is Wednesday, not Tuesday), the first hike since July 2023, with EY and Nationwide formally switching calls. Brent fell 3.6% Friday to $103.70 on profit taking but held a sharply higher week, while projectiles struck two merchant vessels near Khasab on the Omani side of the corridor and Hormuz transits fell to seven a day (flydubai ↗ · FG ↗ · QZ ↗).

Why it matters: two commercial clocks started Friday. First, a three aircraft startup selling into 125+ destinations will not have offline sales coverage everywhere: pitch CargoCrew for representation in flydubai's thin markets before 1 October. Second, SolitAir committing three freighters to flydubai removes regional narrowbody charter capacity from the open market from October: lock Q4 charters and BSAs on Dubai to Africa and CIS lanes this week, before the capacity disappears and before Monday's fuel print resets pricing.
