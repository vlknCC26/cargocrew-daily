The Big Story · The War Reaches UAE Assets

DRONES HIT TWO ADNOC TANKERS IN THE STRAIT — ABU DHABI POINTS AT THE IRGC

Two Abu Dhabi National Oil Co tankers took drone hits transiting Hormuz on Thursday evening — minor damage, no casualties, but the UAE Foreign Ministry called it a "flagrant violation" and blamed Iran's Revolutionary Guard (AP ↗). It's the first direct strike on UAE commercial assets in this war, and it lands squarely on the Oman "safe lanes" track that had been in its final stages (AJ ↗). In Yemen, Houthi drones reportedly targeted Saudi Aramco facilities near the border.

Why it matters: this is the home-hub risk scenario inching closer — UAE-flagged assets are now targets. Re-run the DXB/DWC contingency check, expect UAE-linked war-risk premiums to jump, and note the safe-lanes de-escalation scenario just got less likely — the Gulf premium holds.
