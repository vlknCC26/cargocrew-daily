The Big Story · The Refinery and the Stalled Table

THE HOUTHIS CLAIM A MISSILE HIT ON RAS TANURA, SAUDI ARABIA'S BIGGEST REFINERY, AND OIL SNAPS BACK ABOVE $115, WHILE THE INSIDE ACCOUNT OF THE TEHRAN TALKS LANDS: NO CLEAR PROGRESS, AND AN IRANIAN ASK FOR A TWO WEEK PAUSE TO REOPEN HORMUZ GRADUALLY

The war found the oil coast's crown jewel, at least rhetorically: the Houthis claimed Tuesday they struck Ras Tanura, the kingdom's biggest refinery at 550,000 bpd, with a ballistic missile, alongside claims on Najran airport and the defence ministry in Riyadh; Saudi authorities had confirmed nothing by early Wednesday, but the claim alone reversed Tuesday's $2 oil decline, Brent printing $115.36 (+1.3%) at 01:40 GMT after settling $113.88 on talks optimism (Reuters via Business Recorder, 28 Oct, impact UNVERIFIED). What Riyadh DID confirm was damage in the capital: shrapnel from two ballistic missiles caused material damage in Riyadh Tuesday evening, after the coalition intercepted three missiles and four drones in the morning, hours after the Tehran talks ended, and two more missiles in the afternoon (Khaleej Times live, Al Jazeera, 27 Oct). And the talks themselves deflated on inspection: The National's inside account says Monday's Makkah pact session made "no clear progress", the only agreement being to keep talking; the three delegations asked Iran to rein in the Houthis, Iran claimed it lacks the leverage and wants any Houthi deal negotiated in Sanaa, and instead floated that the visitors lobby Washington for a two week regional pause, against which Iran would gradually reopen the Strait of Hormuz; a second round is possible, perhaps in Turkey (The National exclusive, 27 Oct, single source, UNVERIFIED in detail). That reading supersedes Monday's Rezaei optimism, formally corrected below. Pezeshkian meanwhile met Putin in Moscow with no concrete outcome reported (The National ↗ · KT live ↗ · Al Jazeera ↗).

Why it matters: yesterday's fast reopening scenario just lost its clock. The Rezaei four to five day implementation line is now contradicted by the fuller account: no mechanism, no timeline, an ask (a two week pause) rather than an offer, and the Houthi file explicitly unsolved. Meanwhile the claimed target set escalated from airports to the kingdom's largest refinery. For pricing: war premium holds, quote validity stays at 24 to 48 hours, and the surge playbook stays ON THE SHELF rather than in motion. If Riyadh confirms a Ras Tanura hit, energy war risk reprices wholesale and jet fuel follows; if it denies it, the tape gives back a dollar. Either way the FOMC decides tonight into this.
