The Big Story · The Track Wobbles

TWENTY FOUR HOURS AFTER "POSITIVE MOMENTUM", THE DIPLOMATIC TRACK WOBBLED HARD: TRUMP SAYS NO DEAL IS NECESSARY AND THE BLOCKADE STAYS, ISRAELI MEDIA SAY NETANYAHU'S CABINET ORDERED THE MILITARY TO STEP UP PREPARATIONS FOR A NEW ATTACK ON IRAN, TEHRAN REJECTED THE URANIUM TRANSFER PROPOSAL AND THREATENED EVERY BASE "FROM WEST ASIA TO EUROPE", AND FOUR MORE TANKERS BURNED IN HORMUZ

Thursday reversed Wednesday's tape point by point. Trump said the US does not need a deal with Iran to end the war and will keep the blockade on oil shipments, trade and sanctions in "full effect" (The New Arab, 12 Nov), a day after "positive momentum"; Rubio framed it as economic pressure working, with large scale military operations "unnecessary for now". On the other side of the cabinet table, Israeli media reported Netanyahu's security cabinet ordered the military to step up preparations for a possible attack on Iran (Al Jazeera, 12 Nov), with Ynet reporting a weekend Trump Netanyahu call described as a "shouting match from hell", Netanyahu pushing to resume strikes while Trump holds the diplomatic line (office denies, UNVERIFIED). Tehran's answer: SNSC secretary Rezaei warned that if the US launches another war, every military base involved "from West Asia to Europe" would be a legitimate target ("No safe distance. No safe haven. No immunity."), and Iran rejected a US proposal to transfer its 60% enriched uranium stockpile to a third country, while adding a new condition at COP13: environmental damage compensation. The enforcement layer kept pace: the IRGC said two supertankers carrying over 4m barrels combined were hit by a "violent explosion" Wednesday night on an unauthorised route, immobilised and on fire, and Iran's army said two more tankers east of the strait burned after striking a naval mine, four ships in one night, Iranian accounts only, no independent confirmation, UNVERIFIED. A Times of Israel liveblog headline adds that Trump would consider it "a defeat" if the Iranian regime still stands at the end of his term (fetch blocked, UNVERIFIED).

Why it matters: yesterday's edition warned that the Araghchi "two days" line guaranteed whipsaw, and here it is: the reopening trade repriced into a blockade and escalation trade inside one session, with Brent up 3.3%. The structure of the risk has shifted from "when does the strait reopen" to "does Israel restart the air war", which is a different and worse distribution for Gulf aviation: a new strike wave puts Saudi and UAE airspace advisories, the 16 to 17 Nov flydubai Saudi return cluster and every reopening dated commitment back in play. The desk's standing rules (war risk in every quote, 24 hour fuel validity, both branch customer notes drafted) are not precautions this morning, they are the base case.
