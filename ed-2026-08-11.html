The Big Story · Hormuz Whiplash

DEAL HOPES FADE: TRUMP DEMANDS COMPENSATION, STRAIT TRAFFIC COLLAPSES AGAIN

Yesterday's optimism reversed overnight. Trump now demands Iran pay "for the damage they've done over a 50-year period" before a deal; Tehran has six conditions of its own. Hormuz transits fell from 15 Friday to 6 on Sunday; Kharg Island oil exports are at a complete halt (SCMP ↗). Cathay has pushed its Dubai and Riyadh restarts to late October (Loadstar ↗). Yet MESA air capacity still rose +5% WoW — Gulf carriers keep rebuilding, now ~20% below pre-war levels (WorldACD ↗).

Why it matters: yesterday's "rate unwind" thesis just got a stay of execution — the ME premium and the Gulf-gap charter window likely persist into Q4. Keep both plans live: monetise the gap now, but don't unwind the de-escalation stress test; this is a headline-driven market.
