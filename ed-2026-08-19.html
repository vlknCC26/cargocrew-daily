The Big Story · Hormuz Standstill

ZERO SUNDAY TRANSITS: THE ADNOC ATTACKS HAVE EMPTIED THE STRAIT

The three attacks on ADNOC-operated vessels did their work: weekend Hormuz commodity transits collapsed to five on Saturday and zero on Sunday — against 31 the prior weekend and 130+/day pre-war (AP ↗). Bab el-Mandeb held at 49 transits with zero tracked Saudi oil shipments. Tehran repeats its conditions; Washington says the blockade can run "indefinitely" — and Bessent's economic-isolation package is due any day this week (OFAC ↗).

Why it matters: sea access to the Gulf is now effectively shut at both gates — every urgent shipment in or out of the region defaults to air or sea-air via Indian Ocean routings. Charter demand into KSA/Kuwait/Bahrain should spike again this week; make sure the desk is quoting same-day.
