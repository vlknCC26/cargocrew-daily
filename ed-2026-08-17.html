The Big Story · India

NMIA IS LIVE: FIRST INTERNATIONAL FREIGHTER LANDS — THE MUMBAI MIGRATION HAS BEGUN

Hong Kong Air Cargo's A330-200F touched down at Navi Mumbai on Sunday — the airport's first international freighter, opening a 3× weekly HKG–NMIA rotation carrying ~60 tonnes per flight (ANI ↗). NMIA expects "a significant increase in freighter activity by end of August"; MASkargo follows in September, Cathay is relocating, and AF/Lufthansa Cargo are still evaluating — with BOM's freighter ban running to May 2027, this migration is now a fact, not a plan.

Why it matters: the switch worked — which means the window for NMIA transition services is open and moving. Send the one-pager today (as planned), target the carriers still "evaluating", and use Aeroprime's execution as the competitive benchmark to beat.

Trade Policy · Final Word

De minimis is "dead and legally buried" — US trade court upholds abolition

The Court of International Trade's ruling ends the legal challenges to scrapping duty-free sub-$800 imports. With the EU's €3 duty already reshaping Europe, both major e-commerce import regimes are now permanently tightened (Axios ↗).

Why it matters: the China e-commerce airfreight model of 2023–25 is not coming back — consolidation, DDP and nearshoring products are the permanent replacements. Position accordingly.
Hormuz · Corridor Without Customers

Map agreed, nobody sailing: no corridor uptake as attacks continue and US readies sanctions

No effective date, no joint statement, no vessels using the route; a bulk carrier was hit Saturday. Araghchi concedes the deal "wouldn't automatically reopen the strait"; Bessent promises economic-isolation measures "next week"; Trump calls the blockade a "wall of steel".

Why it matters: the corridor scenario stays theoretical — premium-persists remains the operating assumption, and the UAE's response is still words, not escorts.
