The Big Story · Middle East

GULF WAR HANDS THE MIDDLE EAST MARKET TO GULF CARRIERS

The ceasefire collapsed on 8 July — and this week Cathay Cargo shelved its Riyadh return (ACN ↗) days after Cargolux scrapped its Dubai restart (ACN ↗). KSA, Kuwait and Bahrain sit largely unserved by non-Gulf freighter operators, while Emirates, Qatar Airways and Etihad absorb the traffic — Emirates SkyCargo just launched its first transpacific round-the-world rotation (ACN ↗) and Etihad doubled Paris (ACN ↗). Houthis added a Red Sea "embargo" on Saudi shipping on 20 July (WT ↗).

Why it matters: a summer-long capacity vacuum into KSA/Kuwait/Bahrain that a Dubai GSA can fill with charters & interim cargo management — but Iran has hit Qatar, Kuwait and Bahrain this month, so a DXB/DWC contingency plan is now due diligence, not paranoia.
