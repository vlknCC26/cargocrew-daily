The Big Story · Sanctions Ultimatum

"WITH US OR AGAINST US": BESSENT THREATENS SANCTIONS ON IRAN'S TRADING PARTNERS TOO

The Treasury Secretary announced Thursday that the US will impose sweeping sanctions on Iran and, crucially, secondary penalties on countries and companies that keep trading with it, framing it as "the greatest coordinated economic isolation" and predicting the pressure will "collapse" the government (DN ↗). But no designations, sectors or timeline were released: OFAC's board shows only minor Iran items on 20 August (OFAC ↗). The package has now slipped three times while Hormuz sits near 1% of normal transits (tracker figures) and Brent holds $95.29, up 40.5% YoY (Fortune ↗).

Why it matters: secondary sanctions are the difference between an Iran problem and a regional trade problem. Any Gulf entity with Iran touching flows, banking or insurance is potentially in scope. Before Monday: screen the client book for Iran adjacent exposure and be ready to show US counterparties a clean compliance story.
