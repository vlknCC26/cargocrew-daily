The Big Story · A Deal and a Drone War, Simultaneously

IRAN AND OMAN AGREE A HORMUZ CORRIDOR — WHILE UAE TANKERS KEEP GETTING HIT

Tehran says a safe-shipping-corridor "map" has been agreed with Muscat after three weeks of talks — but full reopening stays conditioned on ending the US naval blockade, transit fees are unresolved, and Trump mused about declaring Hormuz "a territory of the United States" (Iran Intl ↗, Fortune ↗). Meanwhile a third ADNOC tanker in a week was attacked Friday evening — 15 strikes on the fleet since February — and Abu Dhabi vowed to defend freedom of navigation (CBS ↗). Transits: about 2 vessels on Friday against 130+ pre-war; 65 incidents and 17 seafarers killed so far.

Why it matters: the corridor deal is the first concrete de-escalation artefact of this war — but it excludes the US and coexists with active attacks on UAE assets. Practical read: no rate unwind yet, UAE war-risk stays hot, and Monday's insurance quotes matter more than ever. Watch whether any carrier prices the corridor into schedules this week.
