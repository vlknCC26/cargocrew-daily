After three slips, the package finally has a date: the Treasury Secretary holds a press conference Monday 24 August to detail the secondary sanctions he says will "collapse" Iran's economy, with China explicitly urged to cooperate (WTTL ↗, AJ ↗). Tehran calls them an attack on "extraterritorial sovereignty" (CNBC ↗). Why it matters: tomorrow's designations define who in the Gulf can bank, insure and bunker what. Have the Iran exposure screen from yesterday's desk finished before the announcement, and expect fuel, insurance and Gulf routing to reprice within hours of the list going up.
