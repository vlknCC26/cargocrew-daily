No full ceasefire yet — but US–Iran talks via Oman are underway with "US optimism" (CNN ↗), Qatar Airways Cargo is rebuilding Gulf capacity (2× weekly DWC, 3× SHJ), and returning lift is already crushing ME rates — London–Middle East indices fell 24.9% in one week (TAC ↗).
