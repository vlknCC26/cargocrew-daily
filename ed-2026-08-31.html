The Big Story · Home Market

CBUAE ORDERS AN URGENT FORENSIC PROBE INTO BANQUE MISR'S UAE BRANCHES AS "ECONOMIC OUTCAST" REACHES DUBAI'S BANKS

The Central Bank of the UAE announced a "special and urgent examination" of Banque Misr's five UAE branches on Sunday, including forensic analysis of transactions during the period Washington cited, and said it will "study available options regarding Banque Misr's status in the UAE" if the proposed US measure is imposed. Licensed banks, it warned, must "not expose the UAE financial system to reputational risks." The $1.8bn figure is now corroborated: Treasury estimates the branches processed that sum for 103 companies linked to Iranian shadow banking between January 2024 and June 2026. Egypt's central bank says only USD correspondent transactions of the UAE branches are affected; the FinCEN Section 311 rule takes effect 30 days after the comment period (National ↗ · AJ ↗).

Why it matters: the UAE regulator has chosen to move ahead of Washington rather than behind it, which tells every bank in the country to tighten KYC on Iran adjacent trade now. Forwarders settling through Egyptian banks in the UAE face friction within weeks. For CargoCrew this is a week to sell compliance: sanctioned party screening on every AWB, AED or EUR settlement options for principals, and a written KYC pack. Smaller GSAs cannot offer that.
