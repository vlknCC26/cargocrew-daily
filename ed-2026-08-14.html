The Big Story · Red Sea

THE RED SEA TURNS DEADLY AGAIN — AND CARGO OWNERS WILL VOTE WITH THEIR ROUTINGS

A Houthi missile killed six seafarers on the Egyptian-owned Tihamah near Bab al-Mandeb — the first deadly strike in about a year. Bimco warns of the "psychological impact" on operators, the IMO is urging pre-transit risk assessments, and ships are increasingly concealing AIS positions (AGBI ↗). It lands while Hormuz talks sit in deadlock — though Iran–Oman technical talks on "safe shipping lanes" are reportedly at an advanced stage, a possible partial re-opening path that bypasses the political standoff (Al Jazeera ↗).

Why it matters: every shipowner that abandons Bab al-Mandeb this week creates airfreight and sea-air demand via the Gulf. Push the Dubai sea-air product with a safety-first message today — and re-check war-risk insurance on any KSA/Red Sea-touching legs in your network.
