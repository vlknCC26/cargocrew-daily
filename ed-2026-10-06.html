The Big Story · Day Thirty Nine

FLYDUBAI IS FLYING, DAY SIX, AND THE SALES SEAT IS STILL EMPTY

The launch happened. Since 1 October, flydubai Cargo has had three Boeing 737-800Fs wet leased from SolitAir (aircraft, crew, maintenance, insurance) operating from DWC: Doha and Sulaymaniyah from 1 Oct, Karachi 3x weekly, Kuwait 3x from 2 Oct, Erbil from 4 Oct, a Kabul to Delhi rotation from 10 Oct and a Chennai, Hanoi, Delhi special from the 19th, with Almaty, Mumbai and Nairobi flagged next, 23 tonnes per flight, aimed at e-commerce, pharma, perishables, live animals and DG. The commercial finding that matters: six days into operations there is still no GSA, GSSA or outside sales appointment in any coverage; the capacity is managed in house under SVP Mohamed Hassan and VP Cargo Rashid Albashri. And the market it launched into: wk39 has MESA to US spot rates +70% YoY, MESA to Europe +63%, Gulf capacity still ~17% below pre conflict levels, and Dubai to Europe volumes down 44% YoY (AR ↗ · LS ↗ · WACD ↗).

Why it matters: the pre launch window closed without an appointment, which means the in house model is being tested live, and every week it runs is a week of offline point revenue (Africa, Central Asia, the Caucasus, the network beyond the eight launch cities) left on the table. The pitch changes shape: no longer "be ready for launch" but "you are flying, here is the revenue the current structure cannot reach, and the neutral layer that resolves the SolitAir conflict is still unclaimed." Refresh the proposal against the live schedule and resend it this week.

War Risk · The Two Fronts

Houthis claim Riyadh strikes and warn airlines off Saudi airspace; Tehran calls US talks "meaningless"

Weekend claims: an Aramco facility in Riyadh, King Khalid Airport, the Rabigh refinery (Saudi calls the claims "misleading"). The Saudi backed "Operation Dawn of Yemen" runs 1,000+ strikes. Pezeshkian says the US attacked "three times after talks" (undated, UNVERIFIED), while the Doha channel continues and Araghchi tables a seven day plan.

Why it matters: an explicit Houthi warning against civil aviation in Saudi airspace is a direct routing and insurance item for Gulf cargo. Keep war risk clauses indexed and alternates filed for Saudi overflight.
Money · The Hike That Slipped

Payrolls +29k miss cuts October hike odds to ~25%; December stays live with the 10yr at 5.28%

ISM prices paid jumped to 77.9, core PCE has risen 12 straight months, Goldman moved its hike call to December. DXY tests yearly highs ~102.5; USD/JPY trades the 159.5 to 160.7 zone with 160+ flagged as intervention territory.

Why it matters: stagflation pricing: weak jobs, sticky inflation, long yields at 19 year highs. Dollar strength plus a possible yen intervention keeps FX clauses on every long dated quote.
Trade · The Gap's Good News

The Xi summit cut tariffs on $30bn of goods, toys and holiday decorations included; the A350F flew

The new US China Board of Trade covers agriculture, seafood, cosmetics and medical devices one way; small appliances, toys, holiday goods and car seats the other. Final rates still undisclosed. Airbus's A350F (MSN 700) flew 4h10m on 29/30 Sept; 115 orders from 14 customers.

Why it matters: toys and holiday decorations are peak season air cargo categories; a tariff cut lands exactly as transpacific tech demand firms. The large freighter replacement cycle now has a flying aircraft behind it.
