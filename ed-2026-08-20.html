The Big Story · Freighter Race

A DATE AT LAST: A350F MAIDEN FLIGHT SET FOR 24 SEPTEMBER

Airbus has fixed roughly 24 September for the A350 freighter's first flight, weather and technicals permitting, with certification targeted for mid 2027, first delivery in H2 2027 and "significant numbers" flowing from 2028 (ACN/Reuters ↗). Ground vibration tests and the cargo door and loading demonstrations are complete; flutter testing fills the first three months of the campaign. It is the first hard date in the next generation freighter race, landing while Boeing's 777-8F stays quiet.

Why it matters: every widebody fleet plan from 2028 onward now has an anchor date. Carriers holding A350F slots (and those who missed them) will start shaping capacity and partnership decisions this winter: exactly the conversations a GSA wants to be in early.

Trade Policy

Washington's "shadow transhipment" crackdown puts hub traffic on notice

Fallout widens from the White House report alleging 40 plus countries facilitate rerouting of Chinese goods, with $40bn to $303bn in tariffs evaded since 2018. CBP is building AI enabled origin screening; China, the EC and Singapore reject the claims.

Why it matters: Dubai is a transshipment hub. Expect more origin documentation demands on US bound cargo; get compliance answers ready before customers ask.
