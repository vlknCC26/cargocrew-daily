The Big Story · Three for Two at Sea

IRAN SHOOTS AT TWO US WARSHIPS AND MISSES; CENTCOM ANSWERS BY TAKING OUT THREE IRGC TANKERS

Saturday's escalation stayed on the water. Iran fired ballistic missiles at a US aircraft carrier and a guided missile destroyer; both evaded with no casualties. CENTCOM's answer was economic, not territorial: the IRGC linked tankers Downy (off Kharg Island), Stark 1 (near Jask) and Kylo (Gulf of Oman) were destroyed or permanently disabled. Admiral Cooper's message: "If you shoot at two of our ships, we will impose an even higher economic cost, taking out three of yours." Tehran vowed "more severe attacks" on the US Navy, and Trump kept talking up a strike on the Pickaxe Mountain enrichment site "very soon", but no land targets were hit and no strikes touched UAE soil. Brent had already closed the week near $95, up 6.6%, with US diesel at a record; markets reopen tonight with the exchange priced in or not (CBS ↗ · AJ ↗).

Why it matters: the tanker for tanker ladder is climbing but the "no major strikes before November" logic still technically holds, which means elevated but flyable continues. The commercial read for a Sunday working day in Dubai: every pricing input (Brent +6.6%, the pending IATA fuel jump, Xeneta's ME premiums at +66 to 100%) is moving one way. Reprice fuel and war risk surcharges today, with validity clauses tied to the next fuel monitor print, before the repricing wave hits midweek.
