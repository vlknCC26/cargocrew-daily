The Big Story · Economic D Day

TONIGHT AT 10 PM DUBAI: THE SANCTIONS PACKAGE LANDS, AND AVIATION IS ON THE TARGET LIST

Bessent details the package at 2 PM ET (10 PM Dubai) at Treasury, targeting Iran's financial, banking, aviation, energy and cryptocurrency sectors on top of the naval blockade; Trump brands it "Economic D Day" (Reuters ↗). No entity list has leaked. The lines are already drawn: the UAE has suspended all trade and financial transactions with Iran, while China instructs its firms to ignore US sanctions under its blocking statute and has cut US Treasury holdings to an 18 year low of $633.4bn (AF ↗). Iran granted Iraqi tankers protected Hormuz passage over the weekend and warned that "not a single drop of oil" passes if Gulf neighbours join (AJ ↗). Brent opens at $92.59, down 1.9% on profit taking (TE ↗).

Why it matters: aviation on the sector list means aircraft, parts, leasing and insurance touching Iran linked entities come into scope, and secondary penalties decide how far the net spreads. Read the OFAC list tonight before tomorrow's quotes; the UAE's clean break is also Dubai's compliance selling point. This brief carries a same evening update after the presser.
