The Big Story · Day Twenty

THE HIKE LANDS AND THE MARKET EXHALES: 3.75 TO 4.00%, ONE MORE PENCILLED IN, AND BRENT BREAKS BACK TO $103

The Fed delivered its first hike since July 2023: 25 points to 3.75 to 4.00% on a unanimous 12 to 0 vote, no dissents anywhere. The statement was blunt ("inflation remains elevated... The Committee will deliver price stability") and Chair Warsh's press conference was reportedly the shortest on record: "inflation is too high and has been for too long." The dots pencil one more hike by December, then a hold through 2027; Warsh again declined to submit his own dot. Wednesday closed heavy (Dow down 1.2%), but Thursday is a relief rally: S&P +1.2%, VIX down 10%, yields easing off 5.00%, gold at $4,404 after rising through the hike. The bigger cargo story is oil: Brent broke back to $103.60, down 2.1%, on Bloomberg's report that Riyadh seeks to restart half the East West pipeline "within days", against independent repair estimates of three to six weeks. Trump said he told Warsh the hike "won't matter" (Fed ↗ · CNBC ↗ · TS ↗).

Why it matters: the uncertainty premium is out; the levels stay. Money at 4% with another hike signalled means peak inventory financing stays expensive, so customers will push harder on terms: hold DSO discipline. The oil dip is a quoting window, not a trend: the pipeline "days" claim is Riyadh's, the independent estimates say weeks, and jet fuel is still +101.6% YoY. Lock long dated buys into the dip with indexed clauses, and keep selling the surcharge story before rates catch up to fuel.

Oil · The Restart Race

"Days" says Riyadh, weeks say the surveyors: the pipeline gap prices oil

Yanbu loadings are suspended, Saudi total loadings run ~2.1m bpd (down 70% from January), and dark ship to ship transfers work off Sohar. Bloomberg: half capacity sought within days; Reuters sources say five to six weeks.

Why it matters: if the restart slips, Thursday's $103 is the bottom of a bounce, not a trend. Quote into the dip; do not build budgets on it.
